Evaluation

The problem is the forecasting of the temporal evolution of a combustion system. There are 2 phases:

  • Phase 1: development phase. We provide you with labeled training data and unlabeled validation and test data. Make predictions for both datasets. However, you will receive feed-back on your performance on the validation set only. The performance of your last submission will be displayed on the leaderboard.
  • Phase 2: final phase. You do not need to do anything. Your last submission of phase 1 will be automatically forwarded. Your performance on the test set will appear on the leaderboard when the organizers finish checking the submissions.

This competition allows you to submit a prediction model that must be trained and tested.

Metrics

The score is given as a blend of accuracy, physical consistency and inference time. The accuracy metric is the Normalized Root Mean Square Error (NRMSE), computed independently for each of the 11 physical fields and then averaged. For a given field, over all grid cells and all forecasted time steps:

NRMSE = RMSE(field_true, field_pred) / std(field_true)

where the standard deviation is computed over the ground-truth test/validation trajectory of that field. The NRMSE is first averaged over all time steps and grid cells for each field, then averaged across the 11 fields, then averaged across all simulations in the phase.

The physical consistency is measured by computing the phase and gain error of the flame transfer function. The flame transfer function $ H $ is defined as the ratio between $ \hat{Q} $ and $ \hat{\phi} $, where $ \hat{Q} $ is the Fourier transform of the volumetric integral of the heat release rate, and $ \hat{\phi} $ is the Fourier transform of the input perturbation.

The gain error is computed as

gain_error = gain_error = np.linalg.norm(np.abs(H_ture)-np.abs(H_pred)) / np.linalg.norm(np.abs(H_true))

while the phase error is

phase_error = np.sqrt(np.mean(np.angle(H_pred * np.conj(H_ture))**2)).

The time inference penalty is computed as a sigmoid function that is around zero for an inference time of less than 10 s, and it becomes 1 for inference time greater than 10 s. For inference time we consider the time to predict the entire testing trajectory with a physical time of 1 s. The time error is computed as

error_time = 1 / (1 + np.exp(-k * (time_array - time_reference))) where k =1 and the reference time is 10 s.

Scores

The metrics are converted into scores as:
Score_nrmse = 1/(1+nrmse_global)
Score_gain = 1/(1+gain_error)
Score_phase = 1-phase_error/np.pi
Score_time = 1/(1+error_time)
to ensure that they are bound between 0 and 1.

Overall score

The overall score is a blend of the scores computed as:
Score_global = 0.5*Score_nrmse + 0.25*Score_gain + 0.15*Score_phase + 0.1*Score_time
where a value close to 0 represents a bad model while a score close to 1 represents a good model.

Training time and inference time are also reported separately on the leaderboard for reference and are not part of the ranking score itself, but excessively slow submissions may hit the execution time limit.
