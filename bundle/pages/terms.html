Competition Rules

  • Submissions must be made before the end of the Development phase.
  • Maximum 20 submissions per day, 100 submissions in total.
  • Data restriction: use of additional external training data is NOT allowed. Only the provided training simulations may be used to train your model.
  • Do not attempt to access, reconstruct, or reverse-engineer the validation or test ground-truth trajectories.
  • Teams must consist of 3 to 4 participants.
