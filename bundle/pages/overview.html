CYPHER 2026: data-driven ROMs for reactive dynamical systems

Introduction

Addressing climate change and achieving net-zero emissions require the adoption of innovative carbon-free energy carriers, such as hydrogen and ammonia. While these fuels offer significant potential for decarbonization, they also introduce new challenges in the design, operation, and control of combustion systems. This data challenge focuses on the development of reduced-order models for dynamical combustion processes. The objective is to create fast, accurate, and reliable predictive models that can support the forecasting, monitoring, and control of real-world combustion systems operating with next-generation fuels.

Challenge description

The goal of this data challenge is to build a reduced-order model of a dynamical reactive system. Participants will be provided with a dataset containing multiple snapshots of a 2D laminar flame with changing boundary conditions. The model provided by the participants should be able to predict the temporal evolution of the system given an initial condition and a time-varying inflow velocity. The results will be evaluated in terms of accuracy (NRMSE), inference time, and physical consistency.
