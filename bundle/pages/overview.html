CYPHER challenge 2026: data-driven reduced-order modeling of reactive dynamical systems

Introduction

Something about the need of surrogate models for combustion systems.

Challenge description

The goal is to build a reduced-order model of a dynamical reactive system. Participants will be provided with a dataset containing multiple snapshots of a 2D laminar flame with changing boundary conditions. The model provided by the participants should be able to predict the temporal evolution of the system given an initial condition and a time-varying inflow velocity. The results will be evaluated in terms of accuracy (RMSE), training and inference cost, and physical consistency.
